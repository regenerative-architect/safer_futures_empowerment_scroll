# Safety and Scope

This bundle is designed for empowerment, not surveillance or diagnosis.

## Guardrails
- No hidden telemetry.
- No account requirement.
- No precise location collection.
- No public stranger chat.
- No automated contacting of parents, schools, police, employers or services.
- No political endorsements or voting recommendations.
- No personalized medical diagnosis or investment recommendations.
- No procedural drug-use instructions.
- No features are gated behind XP.

## Emergency limitation
This toolkit is not an emergency service. Immediate danger, inability to stay safe, unconsciousness, severe breathing difficulty or other urgent situations require appropriate local emergency/trusted professional assistance.

## Youth privacy
Do not store passwords, recovery codes, government identifiers, precise home/school locations, detailed medical records or secrets in reflection fields.

## Anti-inversion review
Before adding a "safety" feature, ask:
1. Can it be used to monitor or punish the young person?
2. Does it collect more data than necessary?
3. Can the user see and undo what it changed?
4. Does it expose private information to adults/peers without consent?
5. Does it mistake compliance for wellbeing?
6. Is there a non-digital or lower-data alternative?
