# Next-Gen Safeguarding & Empowerment OS — Static Guide

If JavaScript is unavailable, use these ten starting questions:

1. Mental health: What is the smallest safe action that lowers load right now, and who could help if it gets worse?
2. Digital safety: If this account or post went wrong, what is the recovery path?
3. Environment: What nearby place can I improve through a reversible, permitted project?
4. Money: What comes in, what must go out, and what goal is worth protecting?
5. Career: What 2–10 hour project would let me test this field in reality?
6. Belonging: Where are my safe people, repeatable groups and routes to help?
7. Substances/health: What is my exit plan before a pressured situation happens?
8. Civics: Which claims are facts, which are values, and what primary process/source governs the issue?
9. Creativity: What can I make privately in ten minutes before deciding whether to share?
10. Problem-solving: What is the smallest reversible experiment that could teach me something?

Use the pattern: **observe → reduce risk → take one action → involve support → review → stop/adapt/continue**.
